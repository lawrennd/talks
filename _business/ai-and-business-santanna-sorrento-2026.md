abstract: |
  Artificial intelligence is changing how firms operate and compete — not only
  through new products, but by reshaping information flows, decision rights, and
  the boundary between what organisations automate and what they keep under human
  judgment. Drawing on *The Atomic Human* and experience across research, industry,
  and policy, this lecture gives undergraduate business students a clear model of
  what machine intelligence is and is not, then applies that model to international
  competition: where AI creates advantage, where it creates new forms of risk and
  debt, and how firms retain the human capabilities that still decide outcomes.
