abstract: |
  In 1896 Marconi filed his first patent in London and made communication
  wireless and scalable. One hundred and thirty years on, machines do not only
  transmit messages: they summarise, recommend, predict, and *decide*. Drawing
  on *Il nucleo umano* (*The Atomic Human*), this speech frames AI as
  information infrastructure, and asks what remains uniquely human when
  imitation is cheap: trust, accountability, and the cultural judgement that
  still decides what we should transmit — and what we should trust.
