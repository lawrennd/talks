title: "Jaynes' World"
subtitle: "An Entropy-Based Information Game"
abstract: |
  Jaynes' maximum entropy is a widely used approach for statistical inference. It has its origins in the second law of thermodynamics. In this work we engage in a deeper exploration of the approach by developing a maximum entropy game, where the natural parameters of a system have their entropy instataneously maximised. We show that the game has a natural origin configuration that can deals with precision-capacity trade-offs through an uncertainty principle. We explore the emergent physics of the game from this origin.
