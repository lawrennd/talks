  Artificial intelligence is reshaping how firms create value — and how
  investors should read that change. Drawing on *The Atomic Human* and
  experience across research, industry, and policy, this briefing gives
  investors a clear model of what machine intelligence is and is not, then
  applies that model to portfolio questions: where AI creates durable
  advantage, where superficial automation hollows out expertise, and how
  intellectual and agentic debt accumulate when organisations ship faster
  than they can explain. The talk introduces the *judgment layer* — the
  organisational interface where human authority still owns consequence —
  and closes with what that implies for diligence and value creation.

portfolio company has an AI roadmap. It is whether the firm has preserved a
judgment layer — clear authority, models of the situation, and accountability —
as it automates. Where that layer is missing, agentic and intellectual debt
accumulate even as headline productivity rises.}
