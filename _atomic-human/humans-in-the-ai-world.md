abstract: |
  As we enter an era machines are mimicking tasks that were traditional undertaken by humans, CHROs are facing fundamental transformations in organisations both in the roles of the individuals and the form of culture. Generative AI brings new challenges in how organizations manage, develop, and empower their workforce. Drawing on insights from *The Atomic Human*, this session explores how the unique characteristics of human intelligence – our social context, cultural understanding, and ability to handle uncertainty – give us the foundations on which we will reshape the future of work and the CHRO function.
