    url: https://inverseprobability.com
abstract: |
  How do we assimilate artificial intelligence technologies in a way that respects the essence of humanity? The answer lies in education, but the nature of education will also radically change in the face of these technologies. In this shifting landscape we first step back and explore the essence of humanity through the notion of the "atomic human" - showing how our atomic essence is being decomposed and recomposed by digital technologies - and what this means for education.
  
  Drawing parallels between AI as a modern philosopher's stone we argue we are facing a battle between attention capture and reinvestment, and explain how education and skills are at the heart of preserving our human essence within that battle.

\subsection{Essential Skills for the Digital Age}

To thrive in this new landscape, education must foster:

* Critical thinking and information literacy
* Metacognitive awareness
* Emotional intelligence and empathy
* Creative problem-solving
* Ethical reasoning
* Digital citizenship

<!-- Communique -->
\subsection{A Call to Action}

1. Redesign education systems to protect and enhance human agency
2. Build curriculum that values uniquely human capabilities
3. Create learning environments that foster attention reinvestment
4. Develop assessment methods that measure deep understanding
5. Prepare educators to guide students and each other through this transformation
