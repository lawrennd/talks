abstract: |
  As artificial intelligence transforms research and innovation, we face a critical challenge: how do we ensure AI truly serves science, citizens, and society? This talk explores how universities can bridge the gap between AI developers and users, creating a new model of innovation that builds from the ground up.
  
  Drawing on examples from healthcare, planning, and scientific research, we'll examine how the 'attention reinvestment cycle' enables sustainable innovation through collaboration between researchers, practitioners, and communities. This human-centered approach offers a path to realizing AI's potential while avoiding the pitfalls of past technological implementations.
