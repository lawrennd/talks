--- title: "What Comes Next? Redesigning Business for an AI-Enabled Future" venue: "IET Savoy Place, London — British Chambers of Commerce AI Adoption Summit" abstract: |-

A breakout panel at the British Chambers of Commerce AI Adoption Summit (Leading Change with AI). While much public debate focuses on the technology itself, this session looks beyond immediate use cases to how AI could reshape organisations, leadership, and business models over the coming decade. The discussion asks how business leaders can ensure AI augments human decision-making, strengthens organisational capability, and creates lasting value — while maintaining the trust and accountability on which successful businesses depend.
