--- title: "Open vs closed: The debate defining the AI era" venue: "Web Summit Lisbon 2026, Stage 11 — Policy Summit" abstract: |-

As AI becomes a defining technology of the 21st century, a fundamental divide exists in how it is developed, shared and controlled. Broadly speaking, actors in China and the West are pursuing different strategies: open source collaboration vs proprietary models. This panel explores the strategic, economic, and political drivers behind openness versus control, including national security concerns, innovation incentives, and global competitiveness.
