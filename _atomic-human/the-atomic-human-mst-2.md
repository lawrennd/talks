abstract: |
  In this talk Neil will reflect on the political themes that arose in
  the book, the atomic human, where the challenges arise from and 
  where the solutions might lie. 
  
  Broadly speaking we face challenges in the modern information topography
  from both corporate and government entities, the answers may lie in
  an improved form of democractic institutionalism but this in turn 
  implies that the individuals that make up our society from citizens to
  professionals are empowered rather than disempowered by the technology.
